# Session log — 2026-05-13 / 2026-05-14

A two-day session that started inside Phase 4 (paper-1 hardening for the
LLaDA+Qwen substrate), pivoted hard to the original Sfumato vision
(composite AR + discrete-diffusion training from scratch), executed an
overnight 29-experiment T0 run, recovered the signal from a noise-floor
metric trap, ran seven probes against the right axes, pressure-tested
the resulting trade-off claim, and exited with a workshop-grade paper
draftable and a $15 path to a TMLR upgrade.

## Phase A → start (paper-1 hardening, then reframe)

We began by evaluating five candidate experiments (E1–E5) for paper-1
hardening. E2 (cmaj branch divergence audit), G (trace-feature sanity),
and D (Sonnet comprehension scaffold) ran cleanly. D was the
inflection: a +9 pp lift to 84% via Sonnet 4.5 preprocessing. Useful as
paper hardening, but a deployment-irrelevant result (Sonnet alone gets
~96 %).

The user reframed sharply: this whole program has been bandaging
inference on two frozen pretrained models. The original Sfumato vision
was *joint training* of AR + diffusion on a shared backbone. We stopped
the inference work, wrote the Phase-A plan
(`/Users/eren/.claude/plans/sfumato-vision-aligned-rosy-jellyfish.md`),
and built T0: a 60M-300M from-scratch composite model with three
baselines (AR-only, diff-only, paired-separate-trunks).

## Phase B (overnight) — 29 runs, accuracy-noise trap

Provisioned A40 pods via Crucible, ran 29 experiments across 60M–300M,
3k–10k steps, T0 (discrete mask) and T1 (continuous flow ELF-style). At
GSM8K-dev accuracy: composite ≈ paired-separate within noise at every
scale. We were measuring 0–3 correct out of 50 — the binomial floor was
swallowing any composite-vs-B3 architectural signal. The dominant noise
source was the metric, not the model.

## Phase C — measure on the right axes

The user reframed again: "AR-NLL is pure-AR's home turf. What is
composite *actually* supposed to be better at?" We added a held-out
perplexity scorer, then designed seven probes against axes composite
*should* win on: diff-axis NLL, FIM, OOD, position-stratified AR,
calibration, mode-switching inference, and a DiFFPO low-data test.

Headline reversal: **composite beats pure-diff by 0.4–0.7 NLL at every
scale on the diffusion task**, while losing 0.2–0.4 on AR. Net trade
favorable; the joint-training advantage was real, just on the diff head
not the AR head. Probe #6 (1k-problem low-data) showed the AR tax
disappears in the data-constrained regime — DiFFPO confirmed at our
scale.

## Phase D — pressure-test before drafting

A second-model critique surfaced five reviewer-killers: probe #1 needed
a compute-matched control; probe #6's n=3 Δ=−0.007 was below noise;
probes #3/#7 were honest negatives not neutrals; probe #5 was a missing
headline; the narrative looked post-hoc. We ran D1–D5 for ~$10 total.

**D2 (compute-matched)** was the strongest single result: pure-diff at
2× the training steps still loses to composite by 0.69 diff-NLL. Joint
training is structural, not arithmetic. **D3 (data-efficiency curve)**
gave a clean monotone story with a 5.7σ composite win at 500p, a
crossover around 1k, and a small consistent tax above 2k. **D1
(mode-switching at n=3)** landed +1.3 to +2.7 pp depending on
comparison — workshop-grade given the {0, 8, 6} per-seed variance.
**D4 (OOD)** and **D5 (ECE)** confirmed the honest negatives: composite
is uniformly +0.1–0.2 NLL worse on OOD prose and worse-calibrated at
4/5 scales.

Verdict per the Phase-D decision tree: **workshop-grade with a clear
upgrade path to TMLR**.

## Headline findings (canonical in T0_PROBES_FINAL.md)

1. **Compute-matched diff-axis win**: composite 5.42 NLL (3k steps) vs
   pure-diff 6.11 NLL (6k steps) at 200M. Structural advantage, not
   compute artefact.
2. **Data-efficiency crossover**: composite < ar_only by −0.40 NLL at
   500 problems (5.7σ); ar_only < composite by +0.22 at 7.5k. Clean
   monotone trend in between.
3. **Mode-switching inference at n=3**: composite paired-mode 4.7 % vs
   pure-AR paired-mode 1.3 % on GSM8K-dev N=50. Workshop-grade pending
   multi-seed.
4. **Honest negatives**: OOD prose +0.1–0.2 NLL, ECE worse at 4/5
   scales. Included in the manuscript as small documented losses.
