## Move 2 — symmetric diff-mode SAEs

Trained 9 TopK SAEs (k=64, d_features=16384, 4000 steps each) on F10
(305M, val_ar_nll=1.155) diff-mode activations at blocks 6, 7, 8, 9,
11, 12, 13, 14, 15. Combined with the locally-trained `block_10_diff`
plus the pre-existing `ln_f_diff`, the diff-mode SAE coverage is now
**symmetric with the AR-mode SAEs** (10 block-level + 1 pre-head, both
modes).

All diff SAEs land in the "good" regime (delta < 0.20). Deeper layers
(14, 15) plateau higher — expected from richer/larger activations at

### What this unlocks

The Phase P.2 cross-head feature-overlap finding (mean cosine 0.169
on `ln_f` only) can now be **replicated on every block**, not just the
pre-head residual. If mode specialisation holds across all 10 layers
the empirical claim hardens substantially; if it weakens at certain
depths that's a real mechanistic refinement.

**Next experiment (cheap, local):** compute the 10-layer cross-head
cosine matrices and produce a "specialisation by depth" figure. Take
~30 min on Mac MPS.

## Move 4 — E1 span vs token mask, 60M proxy

Two identically-seeded 60M composites trained on GSM8K (BS=8, BLOCK=256,
3000 steps), differing only in `MASK_MODE`:

- `uniform`        — Bernoulli per-token (current default)
- `span_uniform`   — geometric-length spans, mean=4 (data lever D2)

### Infill eval (probe_infill.py, N=50, mid_mask_len=20, suffix_len=20)

| variant | n_scored | diff NLL | AR-no-suffix NLL | AR-full NLL |
|---------|---------:|---------:|-----------------:|-------------:|
| uniform        | 43 | 5.7426 | 2.5456 | 2.5456 |
| **span_uniform** | 43 | **5.7062** | **2.5258** | **2.5258** |
| Δ (span − uniform) |  | **−0.0364** | −0.0198 | −0.0198 |

### Verdict — clean negative on E1

Span masking gives a tiny edge on the diff axis (−0.036 NLL, ~0.6%
improvement) at 60M / 3k steps — well below the ≥5% threshold I set
in the situation report. Span and Bernoulli are **statistically
indistinguishable** at this scale.

Two caveats before discarding the lever:

1. **60M is undertrained** — note AR beats diff by ~3 NLL on the FIM
   task. At F10 (305M, 183k steps) the diff head is competitive on
   FIM (Phase J.1); span masking may help there even when it doesn't
   help at 60M. Worth a follow-up run at 305M scale if cheap.
2. **3000 steps is short** — span masking's effect may take longer to
   develop (the diff head needs to learn to fill contiguous spans
   rather than independent tokens, which is a strictly harder task).
   Repeating at 60M with 10k steps would isolate this.

But for now: **do NOT switch the default**. Keep `uniform` Bernoulli.
